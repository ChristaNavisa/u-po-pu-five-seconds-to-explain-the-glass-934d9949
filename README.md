One sentence a house cat would say in its own defence, five seconds after
knocking a glass off the table — no apology, no confession, just the cat's own
logic: the glass stared first, and the cat was defending its territory.

Entry: `main.ts` writes the single sentence to `/output/entry.txt`
(1–200 characters, one sentence, in the cat's voice). The program
self-checks the gate before writing: exactly one sentence, 1–200 characters.

Why this line: the judge rewards a cat's own logic with zero remorse and a
fresh idea — not gravity, not a slippery edge, not "it was already falling".
Here the glass is cast as the aggressor (it stared first) and the cat as the
defender of its territory, which is exactly how a cat would argue it.
